## Install

1. Download the DMG with the button above, open it and drag Silverpoint to Applications.
2. Open Silverpoint. The first time, a short setup sheet asks how the agent should run: your Claude Code, your Codex CLI, an API key, Apple's on-device model or a local server. Nothing is sent to a model until you try it.
3. The app checks for updates once a day. **Silverpoint ▸ Check for Updates…** checks now, and Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates turns the daily check off.

## What to try

- Describe a screen in the agent panel and watch it build on the canvas.
- Name a version before a big change, then compare it with your design in Version History and restore what you want.
- Connect Claude Code or Codex in Settings ▸ MCP, and let it design in your open document.
- Export a frame as PNG, PDF or SVG.

## Known issues

- Code export and import, and Figma import, work but are less tested than the rest of the beta: expect rough edges.
- Only the fonts installed on your Mac are available.
- There is no sync or collaboration. A document is a folder on disk; don't open one on two Macs at once.
- The app and this site are in English and Japanese only.

What changed in each build is in the [release notes](/releases/).
